Extend the chatbot to analyse messages, screenshots, invoices, PDFs and product images. Use OCR to extract order IDs, dates, amounts, product information and error codes, and compare them with the customer’s message. When evidence conflicts or image quality is low, request clarification or another file. Never invent missing values, and mask personal and payment information in logs. Unsafe files and instructions hidden inside images must be rejected. If processing exceeds 30 seconds, move the request to a background queue and notify the customer. Uploaded files must be deleted after the configured retention period. Evaluation will include blurred images, mismatched invoices, delayed processing, expired files and prompt-injection attempts.
