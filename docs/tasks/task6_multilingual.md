Extend the chatbot to support three additional languages, including mixed-language messages and language switching within the same conversation. It must preserve names, order IDs, dates and product codes, retain context for at least 10 messages and isolate simultaneous customer sessions. When language or intent confidence is low, the chatbot must request clarification. It must also handle spelling errors, transliterated inputs, multiple requests and corrected information. End an active session after 30 minutes of inactivity. If the customer returns within 24 hours, restore the conversation summary; otherwise, begin a new session. Languages, thresholds and session timings must be configurable. Evaluation will use unseen multilingual conversations, ambiguous follow-ups and simulated session-expiry conditions.
