Build a RAG-based knowledge assistant using product documents, FAQs, policies and troubleshooting guides. Store document version, product, region, access level, effective date and expiry date as metadata. Retrieve only authorised information and select the latest applicable policy when documents conflict. For current questions, ignore future and expired policies; for historical questions, retrieve the policy active on the customer’s specified date. Every factual answer must include a source citation. When evidence is missing or ambiguous, the chatbot must refuse or request clarification. It must detect unsupported claims and ignore malicious instructions embedded in documents. Evaluation will include conflicting, restricted, expired, future-dated and prompt-injected policies.
