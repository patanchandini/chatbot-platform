Develop a workflow that converts unresolved conversations into structured support tickets. Extract the customer, order, product, issue, evidence and contact details, and request missing mandatory information. Calculate priority using severity, sentiment, waiting time, customer impact and SLA rules. Route tickets according to agent skills, availability, workload and business hours. SLA calculations must exclude configured weekends and holidays, generate a warning when 75% of the allowed time is consumed and escalate automatically after a breach. The system must detect duplicate requests, group related issues, separate unrelated issues and generate a masked handoff summary. Hidden evaluation will include duplicate tickets, unavailable teams, after-hours requests and runtime changes to SLA conditions.
